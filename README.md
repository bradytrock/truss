# Truss

A contractor operating system for restoration and home improvement: jobs, estimates, invoices, calendar, training, and job photos. Auth, Postgres, Row Level Security, Realtime, and Storage all run on Supabase.

Northline Construction’s sample book is Denver residential work — hail roofs, water and fire restoration, kitchens, windows — plus a thin commercial leftover. Homeowners do not need a company on file.

The source of truth is [github.com/bradytrock/truss](https://github.com/bradytrock/truss).

```bash
git clone https://github.com/bradytrock/truss.git
cd truss
npm install
npm run dev
```

The app listens on port 3847. Everyone signs in with their own account. After you attach a Supabase project, create an account or use an existing one. Truss does not add sample people you can log in as.

Production (`npm run build`) uses webpack instead of Turbopack. Restricted hosts that block extra localhost ports otherwise panic while compiling `globals.css`. Honors `PORT` on `npm start`.

## What you can do

- **Settings** — company name, logo, main phone, office email, website, license, office address, **estimate terms**, and **payment terms** for invoices, plus **Price book** (`/settings/price-book`) to add, edit, and mass-upload the catalog as CSV, plus **QuickBooks** (`/settings/quickbooks`) for the Desktop Web Connector, plus **People**: add a roster seat, copy a 14-day signup invite into this company, restrict someone to their own book, lock a login, or remove them. Each seat has a **profile** (name, title, direct phone, email). The direct phone prints on estimates and invoices for jobs that person owns. Only a company admin sees Settings, under the initials menu in the top right. The Accounting seat can open the QuickBooks connector page from Accounting. Everyone can open **Profile** from that same menu to edit their own contact line. The logo and business block print on estimates, invoices, Pages, and client share links. Default terms are copied onto each new document (and onto invoices converted from estimates) so later edits in Settings do not rewrite sent paperwork. Payment-line dollar blanks stay empty until typed on the estimate; scope, schedule, changes, and contractor language stay locked from Settings. Invites are not emailed from Truss — copy the link. A locked account is signed out on the next load. Restrict keeps the role but forces access to that person’s jobs only. You cannot lock, restrict, or remove the last unlocked company admin.
- **Ask Truss** — header control (⌘.) opens a chat that searches this seat’s book and does the work: new leads, contacts, job moves, estimates from the price book, invoices, expenses and payments (attach a receipt photo), job photos, calendar events, and notes. Sending an estimate or invoice, recording a payment without a photo, voiding, accepting, or deleting a job asks you to confirm first. Runs on OpenAI (`OPENAI_API_KEY`). Without a key the panel explains that and does not write records.
- **Contacts** — homeowners first (no company required), plus adjusters, realtors, and one architect as referral partners. Selecting a person opens an Outlook-style inspector over the list (details, pursuits, and jobs) without leaving the book. **Edit contact** from that window: name, title, phone, email, company, book owner, and referral flag.
- **Messages** — two-way texts with homeowners over Sendblue. The inbox (`/messages`) is a thread list and conversation: search, pick someone from the book, and send. Open it from a job (**Text homeowner**) or a contact. Incoming replies match the contact’s phone and log on that job as communication (activity type Text). Point Sendblue’s receive webhook at `{app origin}/api/messages/inbound?token=…` (`MESSAGES_WEBHOOK_TOKEN`) or at the `receive-text` Edge Function. Outbound still needs `SENDBLUE_API_KEY_ID`, `SENDBLUE_API_SECRET_KEY`, and `SENDBLUE_FROM_NUMBER` on the host, or `supabase/functions/send-text`.
- **Jobs** — one board from new lead through punch (Lead, Estimating, Proposal sent, In progress, Punch list, Complete, On hold, Lost). Company admins, accounting, and team leads can filter the board to specific people’s pipelines (dropdown with checkboxes; the open-job count and dollars follow that filter; the people stay selected when a job is open). Company admins also see **Deleted**: the trash can on the job record requires a reason, writes it to the activity history, and parks the card there so it can be restored. `/pipeline` redirects here. Every new lead opens one costing job; seed, market, type, assignee, sales rep, site, notes, and referral copy onto that job’s Details panel. The card keeps that job code (`BJ081926-A`) from day one. Drag a card when the work moves. Selecting a card opens the field record (site photo, address, crew, tags, related contacts, custom fields). **New** on that card starts an estimate, invoice, interaction, or expense on this job — estimate opens the writer for the job, invoice and expense stay on Paper / Financials, and interaction jumps to Activity. **Text homeowner** opens Messages on that job. **Files** on the job record holds invoices, receipts, and payments as reviewable files (Dropbox-style comments — accounting tags you with @, you fix the fields, comment, and send it back to accounting here), plus attachments from the field (PDFs, insurance docs, contracts, photos) plus **Pages** — branded documents you send out (photo documentation, inspection, completion, claim, or blank). Attach opens the phone camera, the photo library, or Browse, the same way the mobile app does. Each Page has a cover, photo sheets you caption on the letter, and letterhead pages (blank, introduction, next steps, and the rest). Share a client link or download a PDF. The PDF cover is a full-page hero using the cover photo you assign, with company letterhead, job site, optional date of loss and claim number, and who prepared it. **New lead** slides in so you can pick who owns it, then capture **Residential or commercial** (residential proposals are not taxed; commercial includes sales tax), the homeowner, job site, and **Seed** (Podium, Website, Google Ad, Phone, Angie's List, Realtor, Referral, Sales Team, Text Main Line, Past Client, ChatGPT, Social Media). Business development sees every unlocked seat on that list, on each card, and on the record — assigning hands the owner, costing job, and homeowner over; sourced-by stays with BD. When a homeowner signs an estimate, the card’s dollar amount updates to the signed total and the work moves to In progress.
- **Business development** — nav is jobs from the agents they brought in, contacts, and ROI. They see company BD return (cash on sourced jobs ÷ office spend they logged) as well as their own numbers. Assigning a lead (new, job card, or open record) hands the owner, the costing job, and the homeowner to that person; sourced-by stays with BD.
- **Estimates** — Joist-style writer: sections, optional lines, tax, discount, deposit, terms, notes after the total, and a client preview. Attach shots from the job photo gallery onto a line so they print on the proposal, the client link, and the PDF. **New estimate** opens the writer immediately (blank, from a job, or from a **company template**). Pick the homeowner, job site, and residential vs commercial on that page. Build templates under Estimates → Templates, or save any proposal as a template. Residential work is not taxed; commercial uses the tax rate on the proposal. Download a PDF, send a copyable client link for signature, collect a drawn signature in the office or on that link (it is stored on the estimate and prints on the PDF), mark it signed (the job card value becomes the signed total), convert included lines to an invoice. The job owner prints as **Project manager** with their name, title, email, and their profile phone (office line if they have not added one).
- **Photos** — company feed of every job photo, grouped by day, with filters for date, job, who took it, and tag (before / progress / after / issue). Each thumbnail and the open view name the person who took the shot, not the job owner. Click a thumbnail for a larger view. **Open job** is only there if that job is already in your seat; everyone can still see the pictures. Add photos from a job record as before.
- **Price book** — company catalog of labor, material, equipment, allowances, and subcontract packages. Company admins add, edit, remove, and mass-upload items under **Settings → Price book**. Upload a CSV (`name,kind,unit,unit_cost,cost_code`); matching cost codes update the existing row. Estimators drop items onto a proposal; changing a unit cost here does not rewrite lines already on an estimate. `/catalog` redirects here.
- **Estimates** — write a proposal from a company template or the price book (sections, optional work, tax), download a PDF, send a client link for signature. The homeowner’s drawing is stored on the estimate and prints on the PDF. Signing writes the signed total onto the job card and moves the work to In progress. Convert included lines to an invoice.
- **Jobs** — field record (overview, photos, Files, estimates/invoices, custom fields), activity, job photos (upload or URL), and file attachments (PDF and other documents). The same Jobs board holds the lead from the first call through punch.
- **Invoices** — draws and retainage with payment history, outstanding AR, PDF, a client share link, notes after the total, payment terms from Settings, and the job owner as project manager with their profile phone
- **Accounting** — company admin and the Accounting seat get a company Profit and Loss in QuickBooks form, plus invoices, expenses, and payments. **Approve** lives under Accounting (`/accounting/approve`, also `/approve`) — it is not its own left-nav item. It lists every invoice issued in the selected ISO week — week 1 is the first week of the year, Monday–Sunday — and defaults to the week you are in. Expenses and payments still waiting on QuickBooks stay in the same queue. Tag the project manager with @ when you comment or return a file; they get a Home notification and reply on that file inside the job (Files), not on Approve. **Push to QuickBooks** queues that record for the Desktop Web Connector. Invoices post onto **Customer:Job**. Job expenses post as a check or credit card charge on that same **Customer:Job**; office and insurance can stay on the company. Payments receive against the invoice already in QuickBooks — push the invoice first. **Settings → QuickBooks** downloads a `.qwc` and sets the income item, bank account, and credit card account names. Mark entered if you typed one by hand. The connector needs [`20260825210000_qbwc.sql`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bradytrock/truss/main/supabase/migrations/20260825210000_qbwc.sql) (or a fresh bootstrap). Password setup that errors with **gen_salt** needs [`20260825220000_qbwc_pgcrypto.sql`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bradytrock/truss/main/supabase/migrations/20260825220000_qbwc_pgcrypto.sql). Push-to-queue picking needs [`20260825230000_qbwc_queue.sql`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bradytrock/truss/main/supabase/migrations/20260825230000_qbwc_queue.sql). Expenses and payments need [`20260825240000_qbwc_expenses_payments.sql`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bradytrock/truss/main/supabase/migrations/20260825240000_qbwc_expenses_payments.sql). If a job parent is already a vendor, [`20260825250000_qbwc_customer_alias.sql`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bradytrock/truss/main/supabase/migrations/20260825250000_qbwc_customer_alias.sql) lets the connector create a customer named `… Cust` and hang the job under that.
- **Log expense / Log payment** — Create (+) in the top right, or Ask Truss with a photo attached. A photo is required every time, whether or not AI reads the receipt. Images stay on the record. Attaching a photo reads vendor, amount, date, and account via OpenAI (`OPENAI_API_KEY` on the Next.js host — Vercel env vars or `.env.local`). `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is an optional fallback for receipts only. Without a key, type the fields; the photo still saves.
- **Job financials** — QuickBooks-style Profit and Loss on the job (Income, Cost of Sales, Gross Profit, Expenses, Net Income). Accrual or cash. Receipt thumbnails stay under the statement.
- **Calendar** — week view of Truss field events plus each person’s Google Calendar. Link is per seat. Share with your team; company admins see every calendar and whether it is linked.
- **Training** — roofing certification course (companion to *Roofing Construction & Estimating, Revised* by Daniel Atcheson). Original lesson summaries, generated takeoff questions, 70% chapter/practice, 80% exam. Progress is per seat. Team leads and company admin see crew progress and can post training bulletins. Open jobs recommend chapters by project type.

Signup creates your seat only — no sample roster. Existing companies drop Northline demo seats (Jordan Hale, Priya Shah, and the rest) on load and reassign their jobs and contacts to you. **Login As** is only for real teammates you add. Add more people from Settings → People (`/signup?invite=…` joins the company that sent the link).

## What lives in Supabase

- **Auth** — email/password; each signup creates a `companies` row, a `profiles` row, and a `team_members` seat linked by `profiles.staff_id`
- **Postgres** — contacts, homeowners, pursuits, jobs, catalog, estimates, estimate templates, invoices, payments, expenses, schedule, calendar accounts, photos, job files, pages, teams, seats, account invites, training progress, training bulletins, messages
- **RLS** — every query is limited to `current_company_id()`
- **Realtime** — the board and records refresh when anyone in the company writes
- **Storage** — `job-photos` bucket (`{companyId}/{jobId}/{uuid}`), `job-files` bucket (`{companyId}/{jobId}/{uuid}`), `receipts` bucket (`{companyId}/expenses|payments/{uuid}`), and `company-assets` bucket (`{companyId}/logo/{uuid}`) for the letterhead logo
- **Google Calendar** — refresh tokens stay in `calendar_tokens` (RPC only). Metadata and shares are company-visible; company admins can read every linked calendar.

Truss does not measure roofs from satellite imagery or collect card payments. Texts go out over Sendblue when those keys are set.

The estimate writer is specified for the iOS app in [`docs/ios-estimate-writer-prompt.md`](docs/ios-estimate-writer-prompt.md) — data model, totals formula, status machine, and screens.
